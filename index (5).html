Every amplifier is temperature-controlled.

Heat is the fastest way to lose accuracy. Onboard sensors regulate output before the system ever has the chance to drift.

The chassis is machined, not stamped.

Real aluminum holds its shape under vibration and road heat, year after year — the difference customers say they can feel before they even hear a note.

The Epicenter puts the bass back.

Modern mastering and undersized drivers roll off the low end long before it reaches your ears. Epicenter senses what's missing and restores it in real time.

Someone who knows the product answers the phone.

Support isn't a call-center script. Customers who called in the '90s and customers who call today get the same depth of knowledge on the line.

Engineering, not marketing.

The details that separate a system you can hear from one you can feel.

Adaptive digital signal processing

Director-series processors listen to the signal and correct it in real time — tuning the system to the cabin instead of forcing the cabin to adapt to the system.

Temperature-controlled amplification

Output throttles automatically before heat becomes distortion, so the tenth hour sounds like the first.

Machined aluminum chassis

Precision-milled housings dissipate heat and resist the vibration that shortens the life of stamped-metal alternatives.

Epicenter bass restoration

A dedicated processor rebuilds the low frequencies lost to compressed audio and undersized speakers.

Audio analysis heritage

Before building car audio, AudioControl built the analyzers studios used to measure it — a discipline that still shapes every product.

Seamless OEM integration

Interfaces built to work with a vehicle's factory wiring, so a system upgrade doesn't mean tearing out the dashboard.
